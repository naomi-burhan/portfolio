UCI Bank Marketing Analytics Dashboard

Microsoft Excel · Banking Analytics · Campaign Performance

Developed an interactive banking analytics dashboard to analyze customer responses to deposit marketing campaigns. The project evaluates campaign conversion performance, customer characteristics, and contact strategies through KPI monitoring, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive slicers.

Key Insights

  • Conversion rate: 11.5% against a 15% target — below target.
  • Customers contacted more than five times: 10.5% against a 5% target — above target.
  • Unknown contact data: 29.3% against a 15% target — above target.

Business implication: strengthen customer segmentation, optimize contact frequency, and improve customer data management.

Garuda Indonesia’s Corporate Treasury Dashboard

Power BI · Corporate Treasury · 2022–2025

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Garuda Indonesia’s corporate treasury performance from 2022 to 2025, focusing on liquidity, cash flow, and financial stability.

Dashboard Features

  • Cash Position: Monitors cash availability.
  • Operating Cash Flow: Analyzes cash generated from operations.
  • Current Ratio: Evaluates short-term liquidity.
  • Total Liabilities: Tracks financial obligations.
  • Key Insights: Highlights significant financial trends and findings.
